# Pierre - Assistente Financeiro

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Fintech / Personal finance AI assistant
- **What they do:** AI-driven financial management app that categorizes spending, identifies patterns, connects to 100+ banks, and surfaces insights without spreadsheets
- **Target audience:** Individual consumers (Brazil-first) managing personal finances, seeking clarity on spending and investment tracking
- **Page goal:** Convert visitors to free signup; communicate core value (AI automation, bank integration, no spreadsheets) and plan tiers

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Centered, vertical stack. H1 (tight negative letter-spacing): "Uma IA que cuida da sua grana enquanto você cuida da sua vida" sits over supporting paragraph (body text, white on near-black). Below: pill-shaped "Começar agora" button (white background, near-black text).

Visual right: iPhone mockup (0.49:1 ratio, landscape crop), showing app interior with user avatar ("Marie"), transaction row ("Delivery de comida R$ 42,00"), and chatbot UI with Pierre's cat mascot + floating avatar badge ("Galileu" in yellow pill). Avatar circles and text labels float asymmetrically left of phone frame. Near-black background, accent usage on badges (pink for "Marie", yellow for "Galileu").

Button action: link to https://pierre.finance/login (text: "Começar agora").

### Header / Navigation
Sticky top, static horizontal. Logo (Pierre cat outline) left. 5 links center: Recursos, Preços, Segurança, Pierre One. Right: pill-shaped "Entrar" button (white bg, near-black text, full rounded corners). Near-black nav bar with white text.

### Footer
2-column layout. **Left:** Logo + "Seu assistente de IA para finanças pessoais" tagline. 3 social icons (Instagram, Discord, WhatsApp). "TERMOS DE USO" + "CENTRAL DE AJUDA" links. **Notable patterns:** 2-column grid throughout (nav, feature sections, footer). Feature sections alternate image placement L↔R. Pricing uses uniform 3-col grid within tab panel. All CTAs link to https://pierre.finance/login. Near-black (near-black) background dominant (18.6% + 9% coverage). White text primary (76 instances). Accent colors: white (43 instances, buttons + badges), green (28 instances, trust/security callouts), pink/yellow for floating user badges (in hero). Rounded corners on all cards, buttons, inputs; full pill borders on CTAs. Focus-visible states present on interactive elements. Single type family throughout with 4 heading levels + body; largest (H1) uses tight letter-spacing.
